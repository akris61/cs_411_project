Renewable Energy Dashboard

Explore and manage renewable energy data with queries, regions, and more.

How to Use This Dashboard

This dashboard helps you explore renewable energy data. Here's a quick guide:

  1. Run Queries: Select and run predefined queries to view data like renewable averages or CO2 emissions.
  2. Manage Regions: Add, update, or remove geographic regions used in the data.
  3. Search: Find specific regions or indicators by keyword.
  4. Update Dashboard: Automatically populate a dashboard with top-performing renewable regions.
  5. Get Summaries: View detailed summaries for specific regions.
  6. View Changes: Check recent updates to the data.
